Japan Travel Itinerary

This itinerary covers a journey across Japan from Tokyo to Kyoto, Hiroshima, and Osaka. It

includes key attractions, train travel details, and food recommendations for each city. Using a Japan

Rail Pass makes this route efficient and cost-effective.

Days 1–4: Tokyo

Base: Shinjuku, Shibuya, or Tokyo Station area. Highlights: - Day 1: Arrival, explore Shinjuku

(Omoide Yokocho, Kabukicho). - Day 2: Asakusa (Senso-ji Temple), Ueno Park, Akihabara. - Day

3: Meiji Shrine, Harajuku, Shibuya Crossing. - Day 4: Day trip to Nikko, Hakone, or Odaiba.

Transport: JR Yamanote Line, Tokyo Metro.
