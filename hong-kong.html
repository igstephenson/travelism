Hong Kong Transport

Octopus Card – buy at Airport

Buy ‘Tourist Octopus (Sold Version)’ for visitors to Hong Kong. Priced at HK$39 without initial value, load up HK$300 to cover Airport Express. Buy at Hung Fook Tong or 7 Eleven stores, or MTR Customer Service Centre.

Airport Express train (pay with Octopus card)

  • Goes between the airport and Central in 24 mins. Trains depart every 10mins.
  • Airport Express (green) Line, to Hong Kong/Central
  • Transfer to Island Line (dark blue) towards Chai Wan
  • Get off at Wan Chai station, Exit A3 and walk to Hopewell Centre.
